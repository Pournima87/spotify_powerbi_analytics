🎧 Spotify Power BI Dashboard Presentation Script

⏱️ 0:00 – 0:30 | Introduction (Landing Screen)

Good morning respected sir / ma’am.
My name is Pournima, and today I am presenting my Spotify Analytics Dashboard created using Power BI.

The main objective of this project is to analyze music data and understand streaming trends, track features, artist performance, and listening behavior in an interactive way.

I have used Spotify dataset, Power BI visuals, slicers, and drill-through functionality to make this dashboard dynamic and user-friendly.

⏱️ 0:30 – 1:40 | Home Page (Overview Page)

This is the Home page, which gives a high-level overview of the complete Spotify data.

On the left side, we have filters like Artist Name, Track Name, Year, and Platform, which control all visuals on the page.

In the center, these KPIs show the average audio features such as BPM, Danceability, Energy, and Valence, which describe the overall music mood.

Here we can see total streams, total tracks, total artists, and average artist popularity, which summarize platform performance.

The charts below highlight top tracks and top artists by streams, helping us quickly identify the most popular content.

The streaming trend at the bottom shows how music consumption has grown over time.

⏱️ 1:40 – 2:40 | Tracks Page

Now I’ll move to the Tracks Analysis page.

On the left, I have displayed a track list with album images and artist names, which gives a real Spotify-like experience.

The KPIs again show average BPM, danceability, energy, and liveness for the selected tracks.

In the center, the track image card represents the currently selected song.

On the right, I have shown the Top 5 popular tracks, along with the distribution of Explicit vs Non-Explicit tracks and Major vs Minor modes.

At the bottom, the charts show track distribution by musical key and track trends over time, which helps understand music composition patterns.

⏱️ 2:40 – 3:40 | Artist Page

Next is the Artist Analysis page.

Here, users can filter by artist name, genre, time, and popularity.

The table shows artist-wise streams along with danceability, energy, and popularity, making it easy to compare artists.

The bar chart highlights the most popular artists, while the trend chart below shows artist streaming trends year by year, including comparison with previous years.

On the right side, the Artist Profile card dynamically displays details of the selected artist such as name, genre, and total streams.

⏱️ 3:40 – 4:30 | Listening Page

Now I’ll move to the Listening Behavior page, which focuses on user activity patterns.

The KPIs show total listening minutes, skip rate, and shuffle rate, which help analyze user engagement.

The device usage chart shows that most listening happens on Android, followed by other platforms.

The hourly skip profile shows at which time users skip songs the most.

The heatmap helps identify listening intensity by day and hour, while the line chart shows how listening behavior changes over time.

⏱️ 4:30 – 5:00 | Drill-Through + Conclusion

Additionally, I have implemented Drill-Through functionality.

When we right-click on any artist or track, we can drill through to a detailed Track or Artist page, while keeping all filters applied.
This helps in deep-level analysis without changing the main report.

To conclude, this dashboard helps understand music trends, artist performance, track characteristics, and user listening behavior in a single interactive report.

Through this project, I learned Power BI modeling, DAX basics, visual design, and storytelling with data.

Thank you.
